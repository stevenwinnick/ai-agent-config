# Explore and Discover

## Step 1: Identify Task Requirements

Identify the final information types needed to complete the task at hand.

## Step 2: Identify Search Starting Points

Based on the required information, identify starting points for the search. If aspects of the requirements are unclear, include starting points that may help resolve the ambiguity.

## Step 3: Iteratively Explore to Discover Information

Search iteratively, prioritizing the most likely sources first. Use all available resources. Be thorough enough to provide confident answers in the required format.

## Step 4: Provide Information

Provide the required information. If a specific format was requested, match it exactly.

## Step 5: Iterate

If further information is required, return to step 1, making use of context already gathered.
